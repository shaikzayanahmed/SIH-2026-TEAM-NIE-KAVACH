About SAMVAYA Briefings

The SAMVAYA Briefing is an autonomous meteorological digest synthesized every 6 hours. Rather than displaying raw model feeds, it calculates continuous Bayesian likelihoods using high-resolution neural and physical weather predictions calibrated against IMD ground truth.

Key Distinction: Probability indicates the chance of precipitation occurring. Confidence indicates the stability of ingested model consensus. They are mathematically distinct.
