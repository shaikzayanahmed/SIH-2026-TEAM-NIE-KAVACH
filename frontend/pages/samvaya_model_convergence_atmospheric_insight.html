How SAMVAYA Decides

Unlike legacy forecasting platforms that compute a simple static average (such as 25% to every model), SAMVAYA employs an **Empirical Bayesian Weighting Kernel** tailored for localized micro-climates.

1. Ground Residual Feedback

Continuous comparison against the Mysuru automated ground weather station (AWS) computes rolling Mean Absolute Error (MAE). Models demonstrating high variance over the last 12 hours undergo rapid weight demotion.

2. Regime Detection

During calm anticyclonic periods, ECMWF hydrodynamic modeling is prioritized. When rapid daytime convective heating triggers thunderstorm potential, the neural graph model (AIFS) receives dominant weighting due to superior non-linear latency resolution.

3. Elevation & Terrain Calibration

Standard 13km grids struggle with the Nilgiri-Deccan escarpment. SAMVAYA adjusts for localized adiabatic warming and downslope drying effects automatically.

Every weighting decision is logged with its empirical rationale, guaranteeing complete operational explainability for defense, agricultural, and meteorological stakeholders.
