How SAMVAYA Synthesizes Ground Truth

The 5 operational tenets governing our adaptive atmospheric intelligence engine.

diversity_3 1. Multiple Models — No Single Oracle

Atmospheric fluid dynamics contains inherent chaotic sensitivity. Global agencies (ECMWF, NOAA) run different approximations. SAMVAYA ingests all paradigms simultaneously so local forecasts never rely on the fallibility of a single institution.

hub 2. Model-Agnostic Adapter

Different models output differing spatial grids (9km vs 25km) and proprietary binary formats. Our adapter converts everything into normalized high-dimensional tensors projected onto an empirical 0.05° Quad-Mesh terrain matrix.

tune 3. Adaptive Weighting by Regime

Weights are not static. If NOAA GFS historically overpredicts heat in post-monsoon dry air, its weight automatically drops for that specific regime. If AIFS neural graphs master coastal moist fronts, its weight surges for Nilgiri squalls.

analytics 4. Consensus & Honest Uncertainty

We never deliver a false illusion of certainty. When member models diverge strongly, SAMVAYA widens its confidence bounds and alerts civil stakeholders that atmospheric unpredictability is active in the lead horizon.

fact_check 5. Continuous Ground Verification Loop

Real physical ground stations (IMD Agromet networks, rapid Doppler radars, INSAT sounders) continuously test our consensus forecasts 24 hours later. Errors are penalized mathematically, continuously fine-tuning the gating neural weights without human bias.
