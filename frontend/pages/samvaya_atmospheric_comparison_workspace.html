SYNOPTIC GENESIS SYNTHESIS Terrain-Driven Moisture Disparity

Why do these four locations diverge dramatically within 600 kilometers?

The Western Ghats mountain barrier triggers acute orographic forced ascent for incoming Arabian Sea onshore flows. At Mangaluru, warm ocean moisture hits the seaboard producing heavy convective cloud towers and rainfall. As air rises and crests the ridge towards Mysuru and Bengaluru, adiabatic warming and moisture depletion create the classic rain shadow effect. Meanwhile, Hyderabad is decoupled into an independent continental anticyclonic airmass with negligible precipitable water.
